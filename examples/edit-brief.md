## Style

- Rapid hard cuts, punch-ins, crash zooms, and shakes on impact.
- Bold white captions in Bagel Fat One (or the closest fallback), with black
  stroke and keyword highlights.
- Meme overlays timed to the joke, not wallpaper.
- Layered SFX, music drops, and occasional silence before punchlines.
- Running bits and callbacks; end on a button, not a generic outro.
- Keep gameplay and dialogue intelligible.

## Structure

1. Cold open with the strongest 5-10 seconds.
2. Short context: who, what, and why.
3. 5-9 comedy/gameplay bits with escalation.
4. Return to the best running bit near the end.
5. Callback or abrupt button.

## Guardrails

- Work on a duplicate sequence; never touch the original.
- Do not delete media, overwrite exports, or publish anything.
- Show me a beat sheet before cutting.
- Show me a change log after each pass.
- No slurs, hate, targeted harassment, or sexual content involving minors.
- Use only original or licensed assets.

## Deliverables

1. Beat sheet with in/out times and joke descriptions.
2. Rough cut with duration and change log.
3. Style pass: punch-ins, memes, captions, SFX.
4. Review export (1080p H.264).
5. Final export after approval.
